The page of `vaporfit ui` and `vaporfit report` has a new design. The summary shows each tier with a bar, the plugin state, the top blockers and the blocked components. The component list puts blocked components first, and you can search it and filter it by tier, verdict and rule. The page of one component groups the findings by rule and shows the chain and the links to the probes, issues and sources. The page uses the fonts and the colors of the docs site, and the filters are in the address of the page.
